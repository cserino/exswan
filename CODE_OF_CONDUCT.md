# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want ExSwan to be a safe and useful place to collaborate. Be respectful, specific, and patient. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the email address on their verified GitHub profile. The maintainer will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content or restrict participation.

This code of conduct applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when a person represents the project.

## Attribution

This policy is a concise project policy informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
